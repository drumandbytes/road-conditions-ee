## 1. What Teesilm is

Teesilm (roadconditions.drumandbytes.ee) shows Estonian road conditions — weather stations,
traffic cameras, hazards, and road-work restrictions — sourced from Transpordiamet's public
Tark Tee data, plus optional location-based push alerts for paying subscribers.

## 2. Not a substitute for official sources

**Teesilm is informational only.** The underlying data comes from Transpordiamet and may be
delayed, incomplete, or wrong — we have no control over its accuracy or update frequency.
Never rely on Teesilm as your only source for a safety-critical driving decision. Always obey
posted road signage and official traffic control regardless of what the app shows. Do not
interact with the app while driving.

## 3. Accounts

There's no password — you sign in via a one-time emailed link, or an account is created
automatically when you subscribe. You're responsible for keeping access to the email address
tied to your account, since that's how sign-in and account recovery both work.

## 4. Subscriptions and payments

- Paid tier unlocks live camera images, VMS board data, and location-based push alerts.
- Billing is handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details.
- New subscriptions include a 30-day free trial.
- Cancel anytime through the Stripe billing portal (linked from the app); cancelling stops

- Prices are shown at checkout and may change for future billing periods with notice.

features (camera images, alerts) are for your own personal use.
